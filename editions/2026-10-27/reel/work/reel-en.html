Today's coffee news
Lula's surprise win sends the real sliding, coffee with it

Brazil's real fell about 12% against the dollar after President Lula's surprise re-election win, briefly halting stock trading in Brazil. A weaker real makes Brazilian exporters keener to sell dollar-priced coffee, since it buys more reais at home. ICE arabica fell 3.4% to $2.97/lb; robusta fell 1.9% to $3,181/tonne, its lowest since early March 2024.
