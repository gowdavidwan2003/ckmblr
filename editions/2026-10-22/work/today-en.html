Today's news

Exports up 23% on last year

3,85,705 tonnes of coffee shipped out between 1 January and 22 October 2026 (exports plus re-exports combined). The same period last year was 3,13,278 tonnes — about 23% lower. Robusta Cherry alone accounted for 1,62,073 tonnes.

Source: Coffee Board of India, Daily Coffee Market Report, 22.10.2026
Rain returns to Brazil, Vietnam gets more too

Minas Gerais, Brazil's main arabica belt, got 44.7mm of rain in the week ending 18 October — 136% of the historical average. The week before that saw just 20.2mm (48% of average), raising drought worries during flowering. Vietnam's Central Highlands were also forecast above-average rain through 20 October, good for the crop. Separately, there is talk — not yet confirmed — that the 50% US tariff on Brazilian goods could be eased soon. All three point the same way: more supply expected, which weighs on prices.

Source: Barchart / Nasdaq coffee market coverage, October 2026
