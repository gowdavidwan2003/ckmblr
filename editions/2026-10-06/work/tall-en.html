Monday close — arabica at a 3.5-week high, robusta at a 6-week high

December NY arabica closed at 292.55 cents/lb on Monday, up 3.80 cents (+1.32%) — its highest close in 3.5 weeks. November ICE robusta closed at $3,528/tonne, up $59 (+1.70%) — its highest close in 6 weeks. The Coffee Board says the Brazilian real surged to a 4.5-month high against the dollar on Monday — a stronger real discourages export sales from Brazil's producers, which the report cites as one reason behind the futures move.
