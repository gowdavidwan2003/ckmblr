Today's coffee news
Arecanut research body ARDF to widen scope to coffee, pepper, cocoa, rubber

ARDF, which has so far researched only arecanut, has decided to amend its trust deed to expand into pepper, coffee, cocoa and rubber research. The trust also plans an International Arecanut Summit in 2027.

Source: News Karnataka, 29.10.2026
Pepper: Indian wholesale prices firm up on festive demand, Vietnam FOB steady

Indian wholesale black pepper prices firmed as stockholders held back sales during the festive season, tightening near-term supply. Vietnam's FOB export quotes stayed roughly steady, near €6/kg, with no sharp moves.

Source: CMB News (commodity-board.com), 29.10.2026
70% of India's coffee exports carry no Indian brand, says Coffee Board

The Coffee Board says about 70% of the 3.6 lakh tonnes of coffee India exports each year, to over 170 countries, goes out without an Indian brand identity. It is launching an "India Coffee" app meant to bring growers onto one platform to export under a shared brand.

Source: Deccan Herald, 29.10.2026
