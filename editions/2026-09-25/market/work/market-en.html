What's moving the market

Arabica stocks tight, but Brazil's outlook improving — pulling both ways

Robusta and arabica both eased overnight. ICE-certified arabica stocks are near a multi-year low at 254,480 bags, down from 643,341 bags a year ago — supportive for prices. But an improving Brazil rainfall outlook and a USDA projection of a record 71.9 million-bag global crop next season pulled the other way.

Source: doanhnghiephoinhap.vn coffee price report (25.09.2026)
Dollar at a 2-month high — usually a headwind for coffee

The dollar index rose 0.15% overnight to 101.25, its highest in two months, on strong US jobs data and rising odds of an October Fed rate hike — 68.6%, up from 55.4% a week earlier. A stronger dollar usually weighs on dollar-priced commodities like coffee.

Source: doanhnghiephoinhap.vn USD rate report (25.09.2026)
