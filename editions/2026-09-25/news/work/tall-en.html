Assembly asks for a review of the Kasturirangan report

Karnataka's legislature unanimously passed a resolution asking the Centre to revise the Western Ghats eco-sensitive-area line drawn from the Kasturirangan report. It has asked for one year to carry out a fresh survey number by number.

That ESA line runs through large parts of coffee districts — Chikmagalur, Kodagu, Hassan, Shivamogga. Where it finally sits matters to growers there.

Source: Newskarnataka, 24.09.2026
Shivamogga, Chikmagalur, Kodagu keep getting rain

A low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal is keeping rain going on the coast and in Malnad. Scattered rain in Shivamogga's Tirthahalli, Hosanagara and Sagara; Chikmagalur cloudy with normal rain.

Moderate rain is expected in the ghat sections of Kodagu and Hassan. It should ease after 26 September.

Source: Mega Media News, 24.09.2026
