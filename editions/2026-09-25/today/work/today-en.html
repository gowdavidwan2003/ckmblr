Today's news

Wednesday's jump gave back a little ground Thursday

The market had fallen to three-month lows after three weeks of selling, then jumped on Wednesday as traders covered short positions. That bounce eased a little on Thursday — December arabica slipped to 275.35 c/lb ▼0.20%, November robusta to $3,272/tonne ▼0.81%. Both stayed above Wednesday's lows.

Source: Coffee Board of India, Daily Coffee Market Report, 25.09.2026
ICO indicator price moves up

ICO Group Indicator price (as of 23.09.2026): Other Milds arabica rose to 317.05 c/lb, from 313.03 in the previous report. Robusta rose to 162.17 c/lb, from 160.96. The dollar firmed a touch too, to ₹95.94 from ₹95.87 yesterday.

Vietnam news

Exports running strong, but a surplus looms

Vietnam shipped close to 1.7 million tonnes of coffee in the first eight months of 2026, earning over $6 billion — volume up more than 10% on the year, even as export value fell 11.2% on lower average prices, per the Vietnam Coffee Cocoa Association (Vicofa). USDA sees this season's crop from the big producing countries large enough to leave a global surplus of around 10 million bags, which pulls the other way on price.

Source: CoffeeGeography, "Vietnamese Coffee Exports Poised for Strong Year-End Finish," citing Vicofa and USDA, September 7, 2026
