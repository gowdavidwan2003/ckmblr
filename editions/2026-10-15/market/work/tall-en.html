Vietnam's farmgate price tracks the overnight rally

Vietnam's national average coffee purchase price rose to 93,200 dong a kilogram on 14 October, up 200 dong from the previous day, domestic market reports said, as both London robusta and New York arabica futures climbed overnight. Separately, Vietnam's coffee exports for the first nine months of 2026 were up 16.2% in volume on the year, but export earnings fell about 7% because the average selling price was lower than a year earlier, the Vietnam Coffee and Cocoa Association said on 3 October.

Source: vietnam.vn domestic price report, via Archyde (14 October 2026); Vietnam Coffee and Cocoa Association export data (3 October 2026).
Pepper — a second straight ₹100 rise

IPSTA's 14 October report puts garbled at ₹73,100, ungarbled at ₹71,100 and GL at ₹70,100 — all up ₹100, measured against the 13 October report. Wednesday's report showed the same ₹100 rise across all three grades, so this is the second straight session of an identical move. Vietnam's pepper exports in the first half of 2026 ran to 145,686 tonnes worth $940.5 million — up 17.4% in volume and 10.6% in value on the year, the Vietnam Pepper and Spice Association's figures show, working out to an average price near $6,460 a tonne. That is this year's broader run of firm Vietnamese pepper prices, not an overnight development.

Source: IPSTA daily market report (14 October 2026, prior report 13 October); Vietnam Pepper and Spice Association figures, via TradeInt (H1 2026).
