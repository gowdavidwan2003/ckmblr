Exports up 23.1% — Colombia's crop down, El Niño worry for Brazil added to it

India's provisional coffee exports (plus re-exports) for 1 Jan–13 Oct 2026 reached 3,78,399 tonnes, up 23.1% from 3,07,456 tonnes in the same period last year, per the Coffee Board. Separately, the Board's market report said Colombia's Coffee Federation reported 2025/26 production down 15% y/y to 12.65 million bags and exports down 12% to 11.78 million bags — Colombia is the world's second-largest arabica producer. Concern that an El Niño pattern could hurt Brazil's crop next year added to the move, which the report called bullish for prices. December arabica closed up 5.35 cents (+1.83%) at 298.05 cents/lb on Wednesday; November robusta closed up $24 (+0.69%) at $3,483/tonne.

Source: Coffee Board of India — Daily Coffee Market Report, 15.10.2026
