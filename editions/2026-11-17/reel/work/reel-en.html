Today's coffee news
Second elephant-attack death in Belur in three days — Forest Dept orders capture of 10

A wild elephant killed a coffee estate worker in Nagenahalli, Belur taluk, Hassan district, on Monday morning — the second death in the area in three days, after a woman was killed in an elephant attack there on Saturday. The Forest Department has ordered the capture of 10 male elephants from the region.

Source: Siasat Daily, Deccan Chronicle, The News Mill, 16.11.2026
Planning panel member asks PM for special status for Western Ghats

S Mohanadass Hegde of the Karnataka State Policy and Planning Commission has written to PM Modi proposing special constitutional status (a new Article 371K) for the Western Ghats and coastal Karnataka, with a 10-year plan from 2027 that would also review how ESA and CRZ rules apply to farms and plantations. This is a proposal only — the government has not accepted it.

Source: The South First, 16.11.2026
