Robusta, Arabica close lower on Brazil's big harvest

Both futures fell on 20 October. The USDA now expects record global coffee output for 2026/27, mostly from a big Brazilian crop, and Brazil's own Conab has raised its estimate too. ICE's certified Arabica stock is still near a 27-year low, but it has stopped shrinking further, so it is no longer offsetting the bigger-harvest pressure the way it did earlier this year.

Source: USDA Foreign Agricultural Service; Conab, via comunicaffe.com and Barchart; ICE stock levels via Sucafina and ebc.com.
Pepper: no new IPSTA report since Monday

IPSTA has not issued a newer pepper report since 19 October, so today's card repeats Monday's ₹300 rise, not a fresh move. Vietnam's pepper export price, which sets the world benchmark, averaged about $6,508/tonne in September — down 4.1% on the year. Vietnam's 2026 crop is estimated down about 12.8%, which could support prices later in the season.

Source: IPSTA daily market report (19 Oct, prior 16 Oct); Vietnam export data via vietnamnet.vn; 2026 crop estimate via laodong.vn.
