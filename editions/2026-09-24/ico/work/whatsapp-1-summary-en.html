Two forces working on the price this month

Pushing up: certified Arabica stocks in New York are at their lowest since 1999, and NOAA puts the chance of a very strong El Niño this northern winter above 90%. Pushing down: surplus forecasts for next year (Marex 10.5, Rabobank 8.9 million bags) and a record Brazilian crop. What to watch: how Brazil flowers in September–October — next year's crop depends on it.

El Niño fears lifted prices mid-month; rain in Brazil pulled them back in the last week. So the August average ended almost exactly where July did.
