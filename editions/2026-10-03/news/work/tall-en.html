40 more taluks added, coffee taluks among them

The state government has added 40 more taluks to its drought-hit list, taking the total to 217 of Karnataka's 240 taluks. The new list includes Chikkamagaluru, Kalasa, Madikeri, Kushalnagar, Belthangady, Bhadravati, Shikaripura, Shivamogga, Soraba and Thirthahalli — all coffee-growing taluks.

Source: Siasat Daily, 02.10.2026
Monsoon rainfall 35% below normal

Karnataka's southwest monsoon closed at 551mm against a normal 852mm — a 35% deficit, the worst since 1971. The Malnad region, covering Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, Hassan and Shivamogga, recorded a 38% deficit.

Source: Siasat Daily, 02.10.2026
Rain forecast for the coffee districts, Oct 5–6

The India Meteorological Department has forecast heavy rain at a few places in Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, Hassan and Shivamogga on October 5 and 6, with thunderstorms possible at one or two spots each day. Neighbouring Chitradurga and Davanagere may also see heavy rain.

Source: NewsFirst Prime, 02.10.2026
