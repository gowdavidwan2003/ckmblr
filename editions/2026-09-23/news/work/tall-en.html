Today: the Kasturirangan resolution, the state's counsel in the Supreme Court, and the drought list reaching 177 taluks.
1,449 villages
Under the Centre's 7th draft notification, 1,449 villages and 20,668 sq km across 10 Karnataka districts fall inside the Ecologically Sensitive Area (ESA).
Legislature: “Not in its present form”

On 22 September both Houses unanimously passed a resolution rejecting the 27 July draft in its present form. The state says satellite maps do not separate forest from farms, plantations and homes.

It asks that only notified forest go into the ESA, with farms, plantations and homes kept out; a plot-level survey by a committee led by a retired judge; and one year from the Centre to do it. The final decision rests with the Centre.

Source: The Week, 22.09.2026 · ETV Bharat, 22.09.2026
Senior counsel to be appointed: CM

Chief Minister D K Shivakumar told the Assembly the state will appoint a senior counsel to present its position in the Supreme Court on the Kasturirangan notification.

He said the NGT has directed the Centre to decide on the final notification, and that the state is not rejecting the report but asking for a scientific review.

Source: The Daily Guardian, 22.09.2026
Coffee at 3-month lows

On Tuesday (22.09) December arabica closed down 4.10 c/lb (1.48%) and November robusta down $77/t (2.31%) — a second straight fall. The report puts it down to expectations of ample global supply.

Source: Barchart, 22.09.2026
177 taluks
53 more taluks added to the drought list, taking the total to 177 — about 73% of the state's area. The government says it may reach 200.
Malnad rain 38% short

Deputy CM G Parameshwara told the Council on 22 September that the state has had 642 mm of rain against a normal of about 852 mm — 36% short. Malnad is 38% short, the coast 28%.

Losses are estimated at about ₹18,000 crore. The state has asked the Centre for ₹3,500 crore in immediate help.
