Rain and heat together push Arabica picking into October

Sakleshpur grower Lokesh said Arabica is being picked in October, not December, because rain and heat ripened the berries early — more rain risks dropping them. Robusta picking may also move up two months, to December instead of January–February.

Source: Deccan Herald (Gayathri V Raj), 27.10.2026
Forest dept to rebuild elephant corridor by buying private land in it

Forest Minister Ramalinga Reddy said human-elephant conflict has risen along the Western Ghats. The department will buy private land inside an elephant corridor to rebuild it, offering owners alternative land; a planned soft-release centre on 5,000 acres at Tyagadabagi, Tarikere, is still awaiting construction.

Source: Deccan Herald, Chikkamagaluru dateline, 27.10.2026
