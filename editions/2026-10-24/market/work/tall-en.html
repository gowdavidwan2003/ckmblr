Robusta: November contract keeps long positions under pressure

Both Tuesday's (20 October) and Wednesday's (21 October) StoneX reports found robusta rejecting a move through $3,500, sellers in control, closing lower both sessions. Open interest in the November contract stood at 19,631 lots — heavy enough, StoneX said, to keep pressure on long positions. Friday's (23 October) close shown on this card — $3,228, down 1.82% — continues that same slide; no separate report yet covers Friday's session specifically.

Source: StoneX Daily Coffee Report (20, 21 October).
Arabica: midweek rally gives back some ground on Friday

Arabica's ICE-certified stocks had fallen to near a 27-year low, which drove heavy short-covering and pushed the contract to a one-month high of 315.70 cents on 19 October. NRT analyst Vicente Zotti attributed that move mainly to short-covering, saying no confirmed crop-loss estimate from El Niño had been issued. Weighing against it: Brazil's crop agency Conab is projecting a record 2026 harvest of 66.2 million bags, up 17.2% year on year — more supply on the way, which pressures prices down. Friday's close shown here — 307.45 cents, down 1.60% — gives back part of the midweek rally; no report yet covers what drove Friday's session specifically.

Source: Inheritance Coffee journal (ICE stocks, 19 October close); Conab Brazil 2026 crop estimate (as cited in Nasdaq reporting).
