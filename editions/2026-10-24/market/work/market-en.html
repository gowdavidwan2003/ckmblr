Behind the move

Robusta: November contract keeps long positions under pressure

Tuesday's and Wednesday's (20, 21 October) StoneX reports both found robusta rejecting a move through $3,500, sellers in control, closing lower each session. Open interest in the November contract stood at 19,631 lots — heavy enough, StoneX said, to keep pressure on long positions. Friday's (23 October) close here — $3,228, down 1.82% — continues that slide; no report yet covers Friday specifically.

Source: StoneX Daily Coffee Report (20, 21 October).
Arabica: midweek rally gives back some ground on Friday

Arabica's ICE-certified stocks had fallen to near a 27-year low, driving heavy short-covering that pushed the contract to a one-month high of 315.70 cents on 19 October; NRT analyst Vicente Zotti credited that mainly to short-covering, not a confirmed El Niño crop-loss estimate. Weighing against it: Brazil's Conab is projecting a record 2026 harvest of 66.2 million bags, up 17.2% year on year — more supply on the way. Friday's close here — 307.45 cents, down 1.60% — gives back part of that rally; no report yet covers Friday specifically.

Source: Inheritance Coffee journal (ICE stocks, 19 October close); Conab Brazil 2026 crop estimate (as cited in Nasdaq reporting).
