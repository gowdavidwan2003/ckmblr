1. Where prices stood

  • ICO composite (I-CIP) 261.26 US cents/lb — down 0.3% from September. Range for the month 251.28 to 272.34; closed 30 October at 252.32, 3.5% below September's close of 261.60.
  • Colombian Milds 357.85 (+0.5%), Other Milds 334.99 (+1.7%), Brazilian Naturals 294.49 (+1.5%), Robustas 164.19 (−2.0%).
  • New York Arabica futures +1.6% (285.10); London Robusta futures −1.1% (152.56). The gap between the two markets widened 4.9% to 132.54.
  • Volatility 8.7%, down 1.3 points from September's 10.0% — the market calmed a little.

2. Why prices moved

  • Pushed up: US Arabica stocks fell from 0.26 to 0.23 million bags, arriving slower than expected; with the market in backwardation, there is little incentive to rebuild stock; the Brazilian real held up prices through the 21 October election.
  • Pushed down: London Robusta stocks up 29.9%, Vietnam's harvest under way, September Robusta exports up 15.7%; the real weakened again once election results came in (I-CIP −2.8% on 26 October); the 1 October reweighting alone cut the composite by 2.48 cents — a methodology change, not a market move.
  • Pulling both ways: rain in Brazil has helped flowering, but delayed crop treatments and less synchronised flowering raise worry about a harder 2027 harvest; NOAA puts the chance of a historic-strength El Niño through January–March 2027 at 83%.
  • Still unclear: buyers may be restocking early ahead of the EUDR's December deadline, but ICO says this is hard to tell apart from ordinary restocking — it calls the effect "uncertain."

3. Stocks

  • US certified Arabica stocks fell to 0.23 million bags (0.26 at the end of September) — the lowest end-of-month level since January 2002.
  • London certified Robusta stocks rose to 1.13 million bags (0.87 at the end of September) — the highest since July 2025.

4. Exports (September 2026)

  • World green bean exports 10.19 million bags, +5.8% (9.63 million in September 2025). Coffee year 2025/26 total so far: 123.93 million bags, up 1.3% on last year.
  • Robusta exports +15.7% to 4.07 million bags — mainly Vietnam (+26.7% for the coffee year). Robusta exports crossed 50 million bags in a single coffee year for the first time, reaching 50.1 million (+12.3%).
  • Colombian Milds +12.6% to 1.19 million bags; Other Milds −1.7% to 1.61 million; Brazilian Naturals −2.8% to 3.32 million. Total Arabica exports +0.2% to 6.13 million bags.
  • Robusta's share of coffee-year exports rose to 40.4% — the first time it has crossed 40% on record.
  • All forms of coffee: 11.55 million bags, −0.7%: Asia & Oceania −3.9%, Africa +2.8%, South America +2.6%, Caribbean/Central America & Mexico −19.3%.

5. Production and consumption (2025/26)

  • World production up 4.4% to 183.6 million bags (Arabica 104.4, Robusta 79.2).
  • Consumption down 0.9% to 180.6 million bags.
  • After four deficit years from 2021/22 to 2024/25, the market is expected to run a 3.0 million bag surplus this year.
  • South America grows the most (45.8% of world output); Europe drinks the most.
