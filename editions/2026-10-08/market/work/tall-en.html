A big Brazil crop and a surplus forecast have been weighing on coffee this month

The ICO's September estimate puts the 2025/26 coffee crop up 4.4% on the year at 183.6 million bags — a 3-million-bag surplus, the first in five years. The USDA has also forecast Brazil's 2026/27 crop at 71.9 million bags, up 14%. The 30 September report showed ICE certified stocks edging up to 260,654 bags, and the Brazilian real firming slightly against the dollar (5.24 to 5.16) — trade reports describe these as the backdrop the coffee market has been trading against this month.

Source: ICO supply estimate, via Barchart (10 September 2026); USDA FAS Brazil crop estimate (3 June 2026); StoneX Daily Coffee Report (30 September 2026). These sources do not name this as the direct cause of this week's move — it is background context only.
Pepper held steady overnight, Vietnam's smaller 2026 crop keeps the floor firm

All three IPSTA grades were unchanged yesterday (07.10) — garbled ₹72,400, ungarbled ₹70,400, GL ₹69,400. Vietnam's 2026 harvest came in lower on wet weather and aging vines, with output expected down 15–20% on the year — traders say that is keeping the floor firm.
