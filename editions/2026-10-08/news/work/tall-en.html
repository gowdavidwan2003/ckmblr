Today's main story: arabica coffee futures gave back yesterday's rally.
▼10.63¢-3.49%
ICE arabica futures fell to 293.82 cents a pound on Wednesday — down from the one-month high hit just a day earlier.
Brazil's record crop outlook cools the market

Brazil's 2026 crop is expected to be the country's biggest ever — about 67.6 million bags, up 19.6% on last year. Brazil's September coffee exports were also the highest in 20 months.

Hedgepoint Global Markets estimates a global surplus of 8.9 million bags this crop year. Traders say lingering El Niño risk for next year's crop is still giving the price some support.

Source: Trading Economics (ICE Arabica), 07.10.2026
