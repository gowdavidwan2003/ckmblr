Record Brazilian crop keeps pulling futures lower

The ICO's monthly market report for September showed its composite price index down 8.7% on the month, to 262.18 US cents per lb, with the robusta group index down 7.3% — CONAB's record estimate for Brazil's 2026 crop and a larger expected global surplus weighed on the market, Comunicaffe and Business Today reported on 12 October. Low certified arabica stocks were the one factor the ICO said is still offering some support. That report is September data, not last night's session, but it is the backdrop behind the continued softness in robusta and arabica futures through October.

Source: International Coffee Organization monthly market report (September 2026 data), as reported by Comunicaffe and Business Today (12 October 2026).
Pepper — a third straight ₹100 rise

IPSTA's 15 October report puts garbled at ₹73,200, ungarbled at ₹71,200 and GL at ₹70,200 — each up ₹100 from the 14 October report. That makes three sessions running with the identical ₹100 move across all three grades, in the 13, 14 and 15 October reports. Vietnam's pepper exports in September came to 25,152 tonnes worth almost $164 million, though the nine-month average export price is running about 4.1% below a year earlier at roughly $6,508 a tonne, Vietnam Customs data shows — a reminder that the steady Indian rise is happening against softer prices from the other big origin.

Source: IPSTA daily market report (15 October 2026, prior reports 13–14 October); Vietnam Customs trade data (September 2026).
