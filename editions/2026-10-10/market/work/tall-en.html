Robusta up, Arabica down overnight

Robusta closed up $47 at $3,470; Arabica slipped 2.10 cents to 286.95. Vietnam's 2026/27 robusta crop is forecast up slightly at 31.4 million bags, but officials have flagged El Niño risk to the central highlands crop — traders say that's helping hold a floor under robusta. Meanwhile StoneX's 30 September report showed ICE certified Arabica stocks rebuilding to 260,654 bags off July's low, which is weighing on Arabica.

Source: USDA FAS global coffee report, via Reuters (21 August 2026); StoneX Daily Coffee Report (30 September 2026). Neither source names this as the cause of last night's specific move — it is background only.
Pepper still on the 8 October report

Today's IPSTA fetch is still the 8 October report — garbled ₹72,500, ungarbled ₹70,500, GL ₹69,500 are unchanged from yesterday's post, not a new move. Vietnam exported 202,414 tonnes of pepper from January to 15 September 2026, up 15.2% on the year, averaging $6,508 a tonne. Global 2025/26 carry-over stock is put at only around 170,000 tonnes, about 60,000 of it in Vietnam — traders say that tight carry-over is keeping a floor under the price.

Source: IPSTA daily market report — 8 October is still the latest as of today's fetch; Vietnam pepper export data, Vietnam News / ITPC (October 2026, through 15 September).
