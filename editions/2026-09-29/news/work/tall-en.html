Western Ghats ESA: state objects to plantation land inside draft zone

Karnataka CM D.K. Shivakumar has written to Union environment minister Bhupender Yadav saying the draft Western Ghats Ecologically Sensitive Area (ESA) notification includes agricultural land, plantations and inhabited areas. He has asked that the notification be finalised only after the state's physical survey is complete and the High-Level Committee's recommendations are considered. The state says it isn't asking for weaker protection — it wants mapping based on satellite imagery and survey numbers to be accurate first.

Source: The News Mill, 28.09.2026
Central team for drought assessment — Hassan on the list

Three central inter-ministerial teams will visit Karnataka's drought-hit districts from October 3-6, deputy CM G. Parameshwara said. The second team covers Hassan, Mysuru and Chamarajanagar — those three districts together have recorded 4,41,290 hectares of crop damage.

Source: The Hans India, 28.09.2026
