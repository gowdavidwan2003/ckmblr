Rain in Brazil delays the harvest

Minas Gerais got 242% of its usual rainfall in the week to 20.09.2026. Brazil's 2026/27 harvest is running late as a result — traders point to this as one driver of the arabica rally.

Source: Somar Meteorologia data, via trade press (week of 20.09.2026)
Stock picture is mixed

New York's certified arabica stock climbed from a 27-year low of 217,646 bags to 258,415. London robusta stock also rose, to 5,398 lots, a 10-month high. Both markets still closed higher despite rising stocks.

Pepper: Vietnam exports rise
