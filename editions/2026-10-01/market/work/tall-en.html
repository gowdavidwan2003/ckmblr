Arabica stock ticks up off a low

ICE-certified arabica stock fell to 217,646 bags in mid-September — the lowest since 1999 — then recovered to about 247,887 bags. Brazil's incoming record crop is keeping a lid on arabica even with stocks this tight.

Weak Brazilian real adds to selling pressure

The Brazilian real slid to a 2.5-week low against the dollar, making forward sales more attractive for Brazilian growers and adding selling pressure on futures. Brazil's 2026/27 crop is forecast at a record 66.2 million bags, up about 17%.
