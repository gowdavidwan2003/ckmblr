Supply pulling both ways

ICE certified arabica stock is near 223,900 bags — the lowest since 1999. Cold weather at flowering time in Brazil's Minas Gerais and São Paulo has raised frost concerns for the next crop, while Vietnam has shipped past 1.7 million tonnes this year.
