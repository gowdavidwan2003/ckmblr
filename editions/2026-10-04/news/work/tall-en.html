Today's top stories: Karnataka asks the Centre for ₹7,142 crore in drought relief, and a new mapping report on the Western Ghats ESA.
₹7,142.64 cr
Sought by CM Shivakumar for drought relief, during talks with the Centre's team in Bengaluru.
First ₹3,705 crore, then ₹3,437 crore more

While the Centre's Inter-Ministerial Central Team was visiting Karnataka to assess the drought, Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar met the team in Bengaluru and sought ₹7,142.64 crore in relief. The State had first sought ₹3,705.30 crore for 101 drought-hit taluks, then an additional ₹3,437.34 crore after 76 more taluks were declared drought-affected (177 in all). Ground verification is under way in 40 more taluks.

Source: The Hindu, 03.10.2026
Western Ghats ESA: Kodagu, Udupi mapping — much of it already protected

Paani Earth Foundation, a citizen-led group, mapped the Centre's draft Western Ghats ESA notification against Karnataka's own GIS layers for Kodagu and Udupi districts. Much of the proposed ESA area, it found, already overlaps Notified Forests, Protected Areas or existing Eco-Sensitive Zones. The report doesn't give a percentage, but it raises the question of how much land this would actually add new restrictions to.
