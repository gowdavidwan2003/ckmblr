Arabica falls for a second straight day — Brazil supply pressure

Coffee settled sharply lower on Thursday (08.10) on signs of larger Brazil supply and heavy short fund positions in robusta futures — per the Coffee Board's own report. December arabica closed down 3.65 cents (-1.25%) at 289.05 cents/lb; November robusta closed down $62 (-1.78%) at $3,423/tonne.
