Brazil's surplus forecast and rebuilding ICE stocks keep weighing on the background

The ICO's September estimate puts the 2025/26 coffee crop up 4.4% on the year at 183.6 million bags — a 3-million-bag surplus, the first in five years. StoneX's 30 September report showed ICE certified Arabica stocks up to 260,654 bags, rebuilding from a 2.5-year low hit in July. No fresh weekly data has come out between 8 and 9 October — this is still the backdrop the market has been trading against.

Pepper up ₹100 overnight — Vietnam's smaller crop keeps the floor firm

All three IPSTA grades were up ₹100 yesterday (08.10) — garbled ₹72,500, ungarbled ₹70,500, GL ₹69,500. Vietnam's 2026 harvest is estimated down 15–20% on wet weather and ageing vines, yet Vietnam still exported 168,429 tonnes of pepper in January–July 2026 — up 16.1% on the year. A smaller crop moving out faster is what traders say is keeping the floor firm.

Source: IPSTA daily market report (8 October 2026); Vietnam Pepper and Spice Association (VPSA) export data, via ITPC Ho Chi Minh City (August 2026).
