Two forces working
on the price

PUSHING UP

Certified Arabica stocks in New York are at their lowest since 1999, and NOAA puts the chance of a very strong El Niño above 90%.

PUSHING DOWN

Forecasts of a 9–10 million bag surplus next year, and a record Brazilian crop coming to market.

WHAT TO WATCH

Flowering in Brazil through September and October.
